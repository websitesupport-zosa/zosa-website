01 — OVERVIEW

What it is

An MVNO mobile telecom product developed by Zosa for an Australian client — a plan on the Telstra network that turns a monthly phone bill into a growing investment.

02 — KEY FEATURES

How it works

  • Auto-invests 5% of every bill into ASX-listed ETF units
  • Funded from margin — no extra cost to the member
  • Optional Boost and Round-Ups
03 — PLATFORM

Delivery

Native iOS + Android apps, delivered end to end — discovery and architecture through launch and iteration.
